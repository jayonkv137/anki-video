# Strategy: Business Direction Discussion

**Date:** 2026-07-14 · **Trigger:** Jayon's realization that per-user video generation isn't a viable app business model; proposed pivots: content page (Instagram) and/or B2B content service. Cast idea: **Brot & Bier** (+ guest Wurst).

---

## 1. The math correction that reframes everything

The feared "$2.50–3/day *per user*" only applies to **personalized** generation. Our MVP deck is **fixed-order**: every learner starting the 625 deck gets the *same* 10 words on day N. Therefore:

- The whole deck = **~61 episodes** (605 words ÷ 10).
- Generated **once**: 61 days × ~$2.40 ≈ **$150 one-time** — then it serves *unlimited* users forever.
- The MVP pipeline is therefore not a per-user generator; it is a **content factory producing a finite course**. Marginal cost per additional user ≈ bandwidth.
- Per-user economics only break at v1+ (real SRS review mixes, custom decks = truly personal batches).

**The app's real problem is not COGS — it is DISTRIBUTION.** One user (Jayon) will use it. That is Jayon's actual diagnosis, and it's correct.

## 2. Why broadcast (content page) flips the economics

Generation cost is fixed per content-unit; an app shows each unit to ~1 person, a feed shows it to N. At ~$2.40/minute of finished animated content, COGS is near-zero for a content business (human animation: hundreds of €/minute). Supporting evidence: engineered AI characters demonstrably work as a format ([Glorb case, TheSoul Publishing — absurdist 3D character built for meme replication](https://wayin.ai/blog/ai-influencers-on-tiktok/)); AI-generated content averages ~8.7% engagement vs ~4.5% for human creators on TikTok (same source); the comprehensible-input audience is proven at scale (Dreaming Spanish, Easy German — see RESEARCH_efficacy_and_competitors.md).

## 3. Brot & Bier — honest assessment of the cast idea

**Strong, and not just creatively:**
- **Technical:** objects are massively easier for AI-video character consistency than humans (a bread loaf and a beer stein look the same in every generation; no uncanny valley, no face drift). Also NO lip-sync realism expectation — stylized mouths or pure narration both read as intentional. This dissolves the German-lip-sync risk from RESEARCH_video_generation.md.
- **Brand:** instantly legible German cultural iconography; meme-able; guest characters (Wurst!) = running-gag structure; format variety (podcast, adventure, satire/news-roast) = sustainable daily content without creative burnout.
- **Pedagogical:** recurring duo = the parasocial + fixed-schema benefits already locked in RESEARCH_story_design.md. Two speakers = natural dialogue = the abstract-word escape hatch.
- **Positioning:** self-aware "AI slop but make it useful" is exactly the current cultural moment — owning the aesthetic beats hiding it (also matches the HfK course philosophy: own the limitations).

**Risks, honestly:** platform algorithm dependence; AI-content saturation/backlash cycles; German-learning niche is smaller than English-learning; monetization lags audience by 6–12 months (sponsors/affiliates from language apps are the realistic first revenue — that audience has high advertiser value); daily posting discipline costs Jayon time even when the pipeline is automated.

## 4. B2B service (influencers/platforms) — honest assessment

- **Real demand surface:** language teachers/influencers cannot produce animation; per-video pricing (€20–50) vs COGS (~$2–5) leaves real margin.
- **But:** services = selling time; custom demands; sales effort; the tools are commoditizing fast (creators will eventually self-serve). Platforms (Babbel-class) = long procurement cycles, not a solo-student play.
- **Cheapest validation:** make ONE great sample from a micro-influencer's own lesson content and DM it. Better yet: let the public page do the selling — visible output generates inbound "how do you make these / can you make them for me."

## 5. Synthesis — these are one plan, not three

All three options run on the SAME pipeline; they differ only in distribution. Sequence, not choice:

1. **Build the MVP exactly as locked** (B3→B7). It's the learning goal AND the content factory. Nothing in the build plan changes.
2. **Adopt Brot & Bier as the official cast at B3** (the cast decision was already scheduled for B3 — Lena+Bruno was explicitly placeholder). Serves the app AND the page identically.
3. **Post pipeline outputs to an Instagram/TikTok page** as a zero-extra-cost experiment once B5 produces watchable episodes. The page is also the strongest possible portfolio artifact for Jayon's gen-AI job search in Germany.
4. **B2B stays opportunistic** — respond to inbound; optionally test one influencer DM after the page has 3–5 good posts.
5. **The app** remains the proof-of-concept + Jayon's own learning tool + potential premium layer later ("learn WITH Brot & Bier" — the $150 course library + recall app), IF the page builds an audience that asks for it.

**Decision recorded:** MVP scope unchanged. Pending Jayon's call: make Brot & Bier the B3 cast.
