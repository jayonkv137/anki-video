# BLUEPRINT — STORY_SYSTEM.md (design brief, before writing)

> **Status: FOR JAYON'S CONFIRMATION (2026-07-29).** What the document will contain, **exactly where every part comes from**, who uses it, and what measurably changes because it exists. Nothing written to canon yet.

---

## 1 · The one job
> **Given a lesson, the story so far, and a cast — how do I arrive at a scene worth filming?**

That is the only question this document answers. It is **not** a story template, **not** a beat sheet, and **not** a set of rules a writer must satisfy. It is a **method plus a toolkit**, carried by the agent so Jayon doesn't have to.

## 3 · The proposed spine (9 sections)
1. **The method — reverse scenario generation.** Structure → the situation that genuinely demands it → the scene. With the acceptance test: *would a real person in this situation naturally produce this structure more than once?* If no, it's the wrong situation — change the situation, never bend the grammar.
2. **The three inputs** — the lesson atoms, the story so far, the cast — and what to do when they pull against each other.
3. **The load-balance law.** New grammar ⇒ simple plot, familiar place. Big story beat ⇒ language the audience already owns. A planning rule, not a style note.
4. **Engines.** The mystery, the misunderstanding, the journey — deployable devices that manufacture repetition without repeating.
5. **Scene craft.** The camera explains the dialogue; the target structure must be tied to visible action; humour in the situation; plain language; one legible environment.
6. **The 30-second block.** Its anatomy, its dialogue budget, the standalone rule, the hook.
7. **Optional shapes.** The escalation grammar and the five typologies, explicitly as offers.
8. **Arc & continuity.** How a module advances a journey; planting and paying off; why continuity is pedagogy.
9. **Variety & failure modes.** How not to make the same episode 170 times, and the five ways this goes wrong.

| **Director/Overseer** | can only check canon consistency | can answer *"is this edit consistent with how we build stories?"* |

## 5 · The difference, concretely
**Atom `A2.2.3` — "Wo? In der Stadt"** (location = Dative, the minimal pair against direction = Accusative).

- **Without the method:** the agent invents a scene where someone is in the city and says they are in the city. The grammar is arbitrary, nothing in the situation *requires* it, and the learner sees a rule being demonstrated rather than language being used.
- **With reverse scenario generation:** first ask *what real situation forces a person to repeatedly distinguish where something **is** from where something **goes**?* → giving directions to someone who is moving · a delivery arriving · packing a bag · **someone lost on the phone describing where they are while the other says where to go.** → The scene writes itself: Müller lost somewhere, on the phone, Kati directing him. Every single line *needs* wo/wohin. The grammar is invisible because the situation genuinely demands it — which is precisely why it gets learned.

That is the whole document in one example.

## 6 · HARD vs SOFT
- **HARD (few):** the stereotype is never explained in dialogue · no fourth wall / no character explains grammar · one legible environment per episode · the hook reads muted · scene density stays inside model limits.
- **SOFT (everything else):** the method, the engines, the shapes, the craft guidance, the variety levers. The agent uses these to **suggest better**; QC flags, never blocks.

## 7 · Deliberately NOT included
- **No modal-particle spine.** `RESEARCH_cocreation_system_design.md` §6 proposes modal particles as *the* curriculum; our curriculum is now the locked A1–B1 atom list, and particles appear inside it where they belong. Two competing curricula would be a mess.
- **No character↔grammar table** (`SHOW_BIBLE` §5.2 forbids it).
- **No fixed episode template** — the shapes are offers.
- **No prompt-engineering or model syntax** — that is `TREATMENT.md` and the Seedance guidelines.
- **No numbers that belong to pedagogy** (word counts, WPM, exposure windows) — those go in `PEDAGOGY.md`, and this document links rather than duplicates.
