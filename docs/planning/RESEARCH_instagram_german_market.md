## A. The digging playbook (how to find EVERYTHING, from inside Instagram)

Work with a spreadsheet from minute one: `handle · followers · teaching language · format(s) · cadence · monetization visible · notes`.

**1. Hashtag mining — the multilingual master list.** Search each; harvest "Top" (what the algorithm rewards) AND "Recent" (volume/freshness). German-teaching hashtags cluster by the learner's NATIVE language:

- DE/EN: `#deutschlernen #learngerman #germanlearning #deutschkurs #germangrammar #germanvocabulary #germanforbeginners #daf #dafleher #a1deutsch #b1prüfung #goethezertifikat #germanwords #niveaub1`
- TR: `#almanca #almancaöğreniyorum #almancakelimeler` · AR: `#تعلم_الالمانية #اللغة_الالمانية` · RU/UA: `#немецкийязык #немецкий #німецькамова` · FA: `#زبان_آلمانی` · HI/UR: `#जर्मन` `#germanlanguageindia #germanyjobs` · ES: `#aprenderalemán #alemán` · PT: `#alemão #aprenderalemao` · FR: `#allemand #apprendrelallemand` · IT: `#tedesco #imparareiltedesco` · ID: `#belajarbahasajerman` · VI: `#tiếngđức` · KO: `#독일어` · JA: `#ドイツ語` · ZH: `#德语 #德语学习`

**2. Suggested-account chaining (the snowball).** Open any seed account → tap the person-with-arrow icon next to Follow → Instagram shows 5–10 *similar* accounts. Each of those has its own suggestions. Three levels deep from 5 seeds ≈ hundreds of accounts. This is the single highest-yield technique.

**3. The Reels rabbit hole.** Spend 20 minutes ONLY watching/liking German-learning reels (ideally on a fresh account, or Settings → Content preferences → Reset suggested content). The algorithm becomes your research assistant — your feed turns into a live map of the niche, including tiny accounts no list ever mentions.

**4. Audio trails.** On any viral learning reel, tap the audio name → every reel that used that sound → instantly surfaces all the creators chasing that format.

**5. Comment-section mining.** Small creators systematically comment on big accounts (@easygermanvideos comments = a directory of aspiring German-teaching accounts).

**6. Google side-door (no login):** `site:instagram.com "deutsch lernen"`, `site:instagram.com almanca`, `site:instagram.com "تعلم الالمانية"` — per language.

**7. Ranked directories (fastest route past 100):**
- [StarNgage — Top 1,000 education influencers Germany](https://starngage.com/plus/en-us/influencer/ranking/instagram/germany/education) ← literally a thousand ranked accounts
- [HypeAuditor — Top education Instagram, Germany](https://hypeauditor.com/top-instagram-education-germany/)
- [Modash germany micro-influencer lists](https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/germany/micro), Favikon equivalents

**8. Option C — we do it together in your browser.** I can drive your logged-in Chrome (the Claude-in-Chrome extension) at human pace: open hashtag pages, walk suggestion chains, and log every account into a sheet while you watch and steer. (Browsing normally = fine; mass scraping = against IG ToS, we don't do that.) Say the word and we make it a session.

## C. Format taxonomy (what actually gets posted, and the gap)

Observed archetypes across all markets:
1. **Teacher-explainer reel** (talking head + text overlay: grammar trap, "stop saying X, say Y") — the dominant format, totally saturated.
2. **Word/expression-of-the-day carousel** — high volume, low differentiation.
3. **Quiz stories/reels** (fill the gap, der/die/das) — engagement bait, works.
4. **Street interviews** (Easy German) — high production, real CI, few can copy.
5. **Skit/dialogue reels** (teacher plays both roles) — closest human format to ours.
6. **Memes/culture-shock humor** — huge engagement, weak learning value.
7. **Exam/visa/Ausbildung content** (B1 exam, nursing German, job German) — the money niche in TR/AR/HI markets (high intent, migration-driven).
8. **Dubbed/subtitled clips** (movie scenes with German subs).

**The observable gap — exactly where Brot & Bier sits:** No animated recurring-character comprehensible-input SERIES appears anywhere in the seed set or listicles. The market is saturated in *teachers explaining German* and *memes about Germany*, but essentially empty of *entertaining serialized stories IN simple German* — the Dreaming-Spanish-style format that the efficacy research says drives acquisition. Closest neighbors: Easy German (unscalable human format) and meme accounts (no pedagogy). The white space is real on Instagram even though story-TEXT apps (Lenguia etc.) exist elsewhere.

**What to internalize while browsing (the questions that matter):** Which formats repeat across ALL language markets (= universal demand)? Where are comments asking "more of this please"? What do the TR/AR/RU markets have that EN doesn't (exam prep, Ausbildung)? Who is visibly monetizing (link-in-bio courses, sponsors) — and what do they sell?
