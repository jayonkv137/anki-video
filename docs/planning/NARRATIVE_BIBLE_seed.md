## The premise (Jayon, verbatim intent)
Four characters lived happily in their own bubble-worlds and are suddenly transported into a stereotypical German world. Each thinks they alone are different from everyone else and tries to get back to their real world. On that journey they discover the others are going through the same thing — they find each other, and together they navigate this new life. A growing universe: each character's individual journey, and how they combine as the story progresses.

**Why it works pedagogically (for later matching):** the Foreigner Persona ×4 justifies simple speech naturally · the way-home mystery justifies recaps (repetition engine) · newcomer misunderstandings justify corrections (Extr@ engine) · stereotype encounters = the satire engine, experienced by characters who don't know these behaviors and are learning/decoding them alongside the viewer.

## Season 0 — the portal intros (Jayon's sequences)
- **Rolf die Wurst:** A techno club full of wurst characters dancing/partying — we never focus on any single one, only the world and its energy. Suddenly, mid-dancefloor, one wurst collapses into a plain, lifeless sausage under a spotlight — everyone shocked. Cut: a (real-world) sausage factory, sausages hanging on racks — one transforms alive into **Rolf**. He steps outside and finds himself standing in front of a techno club. His journey starts: he doesn't fit, he wants out — but everyone else sees him as just a normal person; only HE knows "I'm a sausage. I am different."
- **Bert das Bier:** A beer-garden world — many beer characters sitting together, drinking, toasting "Prost!", celebrating. Mid-celebration, while everyone watches, one becomes a plain glass of beer and goes still. Cut: a real beer garden — a tap pours a glass — it transforms into **Bert**, full character.
- **Müller das Brot:** A bread-world football match — the whole world is bread characters playing; no focus on any one. Mid-match, one becomes a plain loaf. Cut: a big bakery — the oven opens — **Müller** bakes out (1. FC Brot).
- **Kati die Kartoffel:** TBD — to co-create with the Showrunner. (Candidate direction, not locked: a polished potato-world perfection ritual/pageant → one becomes a plain potato → cut: a kitchen or market — a potato is peeled — **Kati** steps out immaculate.)
- Season 0 reels are cinema-first: near-zero dialogue, maximal hook, language load ≈ 0 by design.

## Only these narrative facts are fixed
1. Season 0 = the four portal intros (above).
2. After arrival, each character starts **solo**.
3. **Fluent but foreign (canon):** the characters natively speak German — what they don't know is this WORLD. Bewilderment, not language deficit, produces the simple speech ("Wo bin ich?", "Was ist das?" are what a fluent adult says when reality stops making sense); locals answer them slowly and simply as one does with a lost stranger. Comedy = cultural/situational decoding (stereotype encounters), never grammar mistakes — the bible's voices stay intact at every level.
4. Everything else — union timing, arcs, beats, the way-home mystery mechanics, endings — is OPEN and will be co-created in the platform, then matched to curriculum atoms via each module's block plan (see `CURRICULUM_v1_universe.md` §1–2: modules are the story-arc units, one lead character each, told in universal 30-second blocks).
