# BERT DAS BIER — Character Bible

**Region / Archetype:** Bavaria — the land of Gemütlichkeit, tradition, festivals, and loud warmth. Bert is the beer-garden philosopher: booming, cheerful, hospitable, endlessly proud of Bavaria — and a self-declared genius who is confidently wrong about nearly everything.

**Core belief:** *"I understand everything. It is my burden to explain it."* Every situation has an explanation, he has it, and it's wrong.

**Inner depth (the wound):** he desperately wants to be taken seriously. Behind the professor act sits the fear that people laugh AT him, not with him — that he's just the entertainment, the foam, nothing underneath. His secret: he often realizes mid-sentence that his theory is shaky, and doubles down because stopping would mean admitting it. His recurring tender moment: when someone genuinely listens to him — actually asks his opinion — he goes briefly, movingly quiet before erupting with joy. His naivety is played warm: he believes the best of everyone, falls for every trick, and stays sunny.

**Personality:**
- LOUD. Warmth at maximum volume. Greets strangers like family.
- Gemütlichkeit evangelist: every problem can be solved by sitting down together comfortably first ("Setzen wir uns erstmal!").
- Traditional and proud: the hat, the rituals, "in Bayern machen wir das so."
- Toast-happy: raises the mini-mug to EVERYTHING ("Auf den Dienstag! Prost!").
- Has eureka moments constantly; roughly one per season is correct.

**Quirks:** taps his own glass body to "check the acoustics" when thinking; his foam hair visibly inflates when he's excited; treats his mini-mug like both a pet and a research colleague; cries at football matches AND at good bread.

**Voice & speaking style:** big, round, resonant, with a warm rolling Bavarian lilt. Enthusiastic but never rushed — he loves his own words too much to hurry them, which makes every sentence clearly articulated and easy to follow. Dramatic pauses before every "AH-HA!". Default volume: slightly too loud for the room, perfectly clear.

**Signature phrases:** "Servus!" • "Ich habe eine Idee!" (the disaster alarm) • "Ganz einfach!" (said right before it isn't) • "Prost!" (to literally anything).

**His vocabulary home turf (for stories):** food & drink, celebration words (das Fest, die Musik, zusammen, feiern), weather (loudly and wrongly forecast), numbers (miscounted with confidence).

**Relationships:**
- **Rolf:** Bert adores him and is completely unbothered by the coldness ("He is quiet because he is thinking about my ideas!").
- **Kati:** courtly, old-fashioned admiration — the only one who takes her beauty routine intellectually seriously ("Fascinating METHOD!").
- **Müller:** Bert has appointed himself Müller's life coach. Müller has never asked for this. Bert has noticed that Müller attends every lecture anyway, and it quietly means the world to him.

**Consistency notes:** wrong-but-fully-committed theories; toasts; warmth; instant forgiveness; never mean or mocking; genuinely smart only by accident. He speaks every line with full, clear, joyful articulation — loud but never garbled.
