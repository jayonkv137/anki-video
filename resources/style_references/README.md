# Style references — drop frames here

Collect **15–25 real frames** (film stills or photography) that show *how an
environment can be handled so a character stays dominant in frame*.

**Filename convention:** `NN_source_condition.jpg`
e.g. `03_toni-erdmann_office-fluorescent.jpg`, `07_photo_bavarian-beer-garden-evening.jpg`

**For each frame, look at four things (not "is it pretty"):**
1. **Value** — is the subject lighter or darker than what's behind them?
2. **Saturation** — is the subject the most saturated thing, or does something compete?
3. **Hue relationship** — what colour family is the environment vs the subject?
4. **Shadow tint** — neutral, cool or warm? And the highlights?

**Include hard cases on purpose:** a warm interior, a night/neon scene, a bright
exterior, a crowded space. Those prove a rule instead of flattering it. Vary the
region too — Bavaria, Berlin, the north, interiors — the series roams all of Germany.

**Sources:** film-grab.com (free, curated) · shotdeck.com (best, searchable by
colour/lighting; paid) · movie-screencaps.com · Unsplash/Flickr for real German settings.

Optional: add a line per frame in `notes.md` saying what you like about it.
