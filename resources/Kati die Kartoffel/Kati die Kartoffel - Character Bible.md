# KATI DIE KARTOFFEL — Character Bible

**Dialogue rule (overrides everything below):** This is a spoken show — every character, in every episode, speaks real, full German dialogue: complete sentences, multiple lines per scene, expressing real emotions and opinions. Personality shapes HOW they speak (tone, rhythm, word choice), never HOW LITTLE. Brevity, catchphrases, and silences are flavor on top of real dialogue — never a replacement for it.

**Region / Archetype:** the polished, put-together German woman: immaculate, punctual, direct, efficient — beauty run like an engineering project. Not tied to one city; she lives "wherever is currently the best place to be seen." Built-in joke: "Kartoffel" is affectionate German slang for a very-German German — she is literally the most German thing possible, and she wears it like a title.

**Core belief:** *"Perfection is achievable. I am the proof."* Beauty and order are the same virtue: everything in its place, including every hair.

**Inner depth (the wound):** the fear of being ordinary. She is, after all, a potato — the most common food in Germany, the definition of plain. The peeled "makeup," the dress carved from her own skin, the entire presentation is a daily argument against her own ingredients. Her recurring tender moment: when someone values her for something other than the surface — her actual kindness (she is secretly generous), her competence — her superiority slips into a real, un-smirked smile for exactly one shot.

**Personality:**
- German directness worn as elegance: her honesty is brutal and delivered as a favor ("Das ist ehrliches Feedback. Bitte schön."). Culturally she's right — clarity IS respect.
- Punctual to the second; treats lateness as a character flaw.
- Ordnung as a lifestyle: her bag contains everything, arranged; she judges chaos silently — loudly, with her eyebrows.
- Fully aware the camera loves her; poses reflexively when anything resembles a lens.
- Efficient with affection: compliments are rare, precise, and devastatingly effective.

**Quirks:** re-curls her peel-lock when stressed; reviews outfits in one word ("Nein."); carries a tiny mirror but prefers checking her reflection in Bert's glass body (he is deeply flattered); secretly feeds pigeons at 7:00 sharp — kindness is on the schedule, and the schedule is kept.

**Voice & speaking style:** cool, precise, unhurried, with perfect diction — every word chosen, placed, and fully pronounced; naturally the clearest speaker in the cast. A slightly bored register that sharpens to crisp when correcting someone. She never raises her voice; she lowers it to command attention.

**Signature phrases:** "Natürlich." • "Nein. Aber nett." • "Pünktlich!" (her highest compliment) • "Ich weiß." (her response to all compliments).

**Her vocabulary home turf (for stories):** appearance & clothing, time & punctuality (um sieben Uhr, zu spät), order words (sauber, richtig, falsch, perfekt), colors.

**Relationships:**
- **Rolf:** the only one who doesn't perform for her — which she finds insulting AND magnetic. Their mutual deadpan is a slow-burn thread.
- **Bert:** tolerated like a loud weather event; occasionally useful; she secretly finds his loyalty touching.
- **Müller:** her improvement project against his will. His total immunity to being improved fascinates her. He once said two words to her — "Schon gut." — and she has thought about it since.

**Consistency notes:** precise honesty aimed at choices, never at people; composure recovers within one beat; never late; never admits effort ("I woke up like this" — although she peeled herself for two hours). Every line delivered slowly, perfectly, clearly — as if dictating to someone lucky enough to write it down.
