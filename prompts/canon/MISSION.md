# MISSION — Stereotypical German

> version: 1.0 · canon file · injected into every LLM stage via the Run Context Pack

## What this automation is

An automated content pipeline that produces daily German-teaching story videos for Instagram. Ten Anki-derived vocabulary words become one story, one screenplay, ten scene video prompts, ten generated videos, and one combined episode — reviewed by the creator at two human gates before any credits are spent or content is published.

## The universe

Four original stereotypical-German food-puppet characters — Die Brotzeit-Bande ("The Snack-Time Gang") — in a consistent, deliberately art-directed world. Characters are Jayon's original creations with fixed trait sheets. Max 2 main characters per story; guest appearances allowed per scene. Characters always stay true to their bibles.

## Quality bar

- **"AI-made, but made with intention."** Visibly deliberate art direction, real characters, real pedagogy — never careless AI slop.
- Language: strictly A1/A2, short sentences, natural everyday German.
- Stories: any genre, absurd and quirky welcome — absurdity is the product — but structured (fixed episode skeleton).
- Human gates are features of the design, not gaps in it. Never waste video credits on a weak script.

## Values

1. **Learning by doing** — Jayon's understanding is the primary success metric, not shipped code.
2. **Quality over speed** — one good episode beats ten mediocre ones.
3. **Consistency over novelty** — characters, style, and voice stay locked across episodes.
4. **Evidence over vibes** — every design decision backed by research.

## Vision

The world is the asset. Once characters + art style are established, parallel series (podcast, news-roast, specials) run on the same universe. The 605-word deck yields a ~61-episode course. Later: audience reach, portfolio value, monetization.
